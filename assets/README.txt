Vlog Studio synthetic demonstration assets

These original, hand-authored SVG illustrations represent imaginary travel
scenes inspired by Indian architecture, street markets, chai, and evening
riverfronts. They are synthetic demonstration material, not documentary footage
of a real trip, customer, event, or exact location. No official brand marks,
stock photographs, remote resources, fonts, or copyrighted video clips are used.

scene-morning.svg   Morning architecture and a walking figure   720 x 1280
scene-market.svg    A warm street market and produce stalls     720 x 1280
scene-chai.svg      Chai on a balcony table                     720 x 1280
scene-evening.svg   Evening riverfront and sandstone steps      720 x 1280
cover-india.svg     Wide travel-series cover                    1280 x 640

The matching MP4 files are silent 12-second looping animations of the SVG art,
rendered at 360 x 640 pixels and 24 frames per second with H.264 video. They are
playable sample assets for the prototype editor, not user-uploaded videos.

All SVG files are self-contained. generate_assets.py is the reproducible source
and uses Python's standard library. The optional --videos command uses locally
installed Inkscape and FFmpeg to render the demonstration videos. It does not
download anything.

Keep the product UI's synthetic/demo disclosure visible when showing these assets.
